Welcome to Mind Arc Games

We're an independent creative studio focused on building a cinematic sci-fi experience with a strong visual identity and a nostalgic and memorable atmosphere.

At the center of that mission is Phoenix Vector. Inspired by shooters of the late 80's and early 90s, its a high-intensity side-scrolling space combat experience set within a larger universe of conflict, mystery, and stellar ambition.

Phoenix Vector

Enter the Aurelia System and engage in fast, precise combat across a war-torn frontier of drifting stations, hostile fleets, and glowing celestial horizons.

Phoenix Vector combines arcade-inspired action with a bold sci-fi aesthetic, layered atmosphere, and a universe designed to expand.
