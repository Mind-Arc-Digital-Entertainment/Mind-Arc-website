Phoenix Vector

Combat in the Aurelia System

Phoenix Vector is a side-scrolling sci-fi combat experience set in the distant Aurelia System, a region of distant space with hostile enemy fleets, luminous worlds, and an escalating conflict.

With sharp moment-to-moment action and bold presentation, Phoenix Vector challenges you to balance speed and firepower while surviving escalating enemy encounters.

  • Fast-paced arcade-inspired combat
  • Strategic power management
  • Distinct sci-fi action and atmosphere
  • Expanding story and worlds
  • An immersive player experience

The Codex

The central command archive of Phoenix Vector detailing ships, personnel, locations, and the evolving conflict across the Aurelia System.
